📘 Learn the concepts

Read these explanations alongside the ray diagram.

1. What is a prism?

A prism is a transparent refracting medium bounded by two inclined plane surfaces. In a triangular glass prism, the two sloping faces refract the light.

2. Why does light bend?

Light changes speed when it travels between air and glass. At the first face it bends towards the normal; when it leaves glass for air, it bends away from the normal.

3. Angle of deviation

The emergent ray generally travels in a different direction from the incident ray. The angle between the original direction and the emergent direction is called the angle of deviation, δ.

4. Dispersion of white light

White light contains many colours. The refractive index of glass depends on wavelength, so colours refract by different amounts and spread into a spectrum. Violet usually deviates most and red least.

5. The important angles

i: incidence at the first face; r₁: refraction inside at the first face; r₂: incidence inside at the second face; e: emergence into air; A: angle between prism faces; δ: deviation.

6. What to investigate

Increase the incidence angle and watch the internal ray. Change the refractive index to compare bending. Then switch between red, green and violet to discuss dispersion.
